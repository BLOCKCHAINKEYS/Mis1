Burger Quest Privacy Notice

Last updated November 19, 2026.

Local-only game progress

Burger Quest is an offline-capable single-player web game. It does not require an account and the game code does not send gameplay, personal details, or analytics to a publisher-operated server. Your cash, restaurant upgrades, orders, and settings are saved in your browser storage on the device you use. Use Save / Menu to export a JSON backup; the export stays on your device until you choose to share it.

What the host may collect

The website hosting company may automatically process standard connection information such as IP address, user agent, and request timestamps to deliver the site and handle security. The final operator must review its own hosting-provider logs, retention, and privacy obligations before publication. This notice does not claim to cover hosting systems we have not configured.

Device permissions and third parties

The game does not request camera, microphone, GPS, contacts, or payment permissions. It ships without advertising trackers or third-party runtime scripts. It uses a browser service worker to store the game files for offline play.

Delete or transfer progress

Export your save before changing phones, clearing site data, or switching domains. Import it on your other device using Save / Menu. Reset from the same menu to start over. Browser settings can erase the local save at any time.

Contact

For questions, contact the website owner through the contact channel published alongside the final deployed site. A verified support address has not been configured in this build.
